❓ FAQ

Common Questions

How accurate is AgriMind's recommendation? ⌄
The current version uses rule-based recommendations. Once the ML model is trained and connected, this section can show the tested model accuracy from your own dataset.
What inputs does the system need? ⌄
You need N, P, K values from a soil test, temperature (°C), humidity (%), pH, and rainfall (mm). Enter these values manually from your soil and weather data.
When does profit data appear? ⌄
Profit analytics only appear after you mark a crop as grown and enter your actual revenue — keeping your data accurate.
Is my data saved? ⌄
Yes — your recommendations, crop history, and profile are saved locally in your browser and persist across sessions until you log out.
